# CaseClosed: The Blind Angle

A short browser detective game in which the player proves a gallery theft by examining records, testing viewpoints and supporting a final conclusion.

## The Unique Kernel
An empty-display image is ambiguous because two displays look alike. The player reconstructs the fixed camera's sightlines against an opaque partition to identify the photographed display, then connects the verified image to timing and key-access records. A false witness account is suspicious but does not, by itself, establish theft.

## Who It's For
Proposed by the agent at the learner's request: someone who enjoys detective stories or escape-room puzzles and wants to solve a short case themselves. Instead of only watching a detective explain the ending, they examine the materials and demonstrate why their conclusion follows.

## The Core Loop
Enter the gallery, inspect an original record, question an account, test the relevant viewpoint, and use the resulting evidence to revise the theory. Finish by choosing a suspect, time window and supporting records, then read the actual feedback and case review. This PoC contains one case; replay lets the player revisit clues, rather than promising fresh generated mysteries.

## Inspiration & Identity
The learner requested a beautiful, cinematic, animated introduction, background music, restrained effects and an interface that is easy to understand. Preserve the existing dark petrol-and-gold gallery atmosphere and readable evidence views. The aesthetic should invite investigation without obscuring the next action. Existing prototype: https://caseclosed-the-blind-angle.windyrhino8195.chatgpt.site/game/

## Why This Matters to the Learner
She wants a distinctive hackathon entry she can understand and explain, with a convincing demonstration of real functionality. Do not promise a win or attribute an unreported personal experience to her.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A player can open the app, enter the story, inspect evidence, test both camera sightlines, record the verified reconstruction and submit a supported theory that reaches the resolution. An incorrect accusation produces a useful explanation. The memorable beat is recognizing that an image of an empty case becomes proof only after its viewpoint is verified.

## The POC Boundary
One fictional gallery theft, three authored witnesses, six source records plus a derived reconstruction, viewpoint experiments, evidence-backed verdict feedback, browser-local progress and user-controlled sound. The target is a complete playable browser mystery with reasoning practice; improved learning outcomes have not been established by testing.

## Later
Additional independently reviewed cases, player comprehension testing, broader physical-device testing and optional constrained live-model question interpretation. Treat educational effectiveness as something to test, not an achieved result.

## Explicitly Cut
- Paid live-model activation: the learner declined paid API use; authored witness responses keep this case playable.
- Dynamic case generation and multiplayer: unnecessary for demonstrating one coherent investigation.
- Real-world forensic claims and moving CCTV: the prototype uses fictional records and a schematic camera still.
- A large replayable game platform: beyond this proof of concept.
