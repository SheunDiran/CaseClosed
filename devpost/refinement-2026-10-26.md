# Investigation refinement — 26 October 2026

This is an improvement to the existing CaseClosed game, not a newly created project. The original playable case and most artwork, music, evidence, reconstruction tools and witness dialogue existed before this pass. No claim of eligibility for a subsequent competition is made here. Any future entry must disclose the original project, previous submission and actual event contributions.

## Changes in this pass

- A rewritten briefing explains the incident, player role and first action.
- Optional first-suspect selection is recorded before investigating and compared with the final evidence-supported finding. The first hunch cannot be changed after investigation begins.
- A context-sensitive next-lead panel points to records or experiments without selecting the culprit. Milestones reflect actual saved progress.
- Three authored deduction challenges require a judgement and supporting evidence. The checks validate inspected sources and experiment prerequisites. A correct judgement without its required evidence does not pass.
- The final debrief preserves the existing resolution and adds a personal investigation summary.
- A post-case experiment explains the consequence of setting aside each core proof record. It does not remove records or mutate the solved case. This is fixed case logic, not generative counterfactual AI.
- A spoiler-free share action uses the device share sheet or clipboard where available. It does not automatically send a message.
- Existing version-2 saves migrate with safe defaults. Unsupported or impossible deduction completions are discarded.
- New assets have explicit browser cache versions so returning players receive compatible modules.

No independent player testing, engagement measurements, revenue, improved learning outcomes or prize prospects have been measured. Native sharing depends on device support. This pass does not activate live model calls. Dialogues and new deductions remain authored and labelled.
